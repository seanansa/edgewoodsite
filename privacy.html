Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit or contact Edgewood.

When you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your inquiry.

Our website links to a separate booking service. When you make a reservation, that provider processes booking and payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Our website host may process standard technical information needed to deliver the site, such as request and security logs. We do not ask for payment details through this website's contact form.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent directly to us, email stay@edgewoodcatskills.com.

Before launch: Verify this notice against the actual analytics, cookie, form, hosting, and booking integrations in use. Update it if additional data is collected or shared.
